September 10, 2026 – January 7, 2027

Venus in Scorpio Horoscopes by Rising Sign

An extended Venus cycle through desire, attachment, value, relationship, and the places where love asks us to become more conscious.

Read for your Rising Sign using Whole Sign Houses.

Venus in Scorpio 2026–27

Venus enters Scorpio on September 10 and begins a journey that will not move in a straight line. She travels through Scorpio as an Evening Star, stations retrograde, meets the Sun at the Kazimi, returns to Libra, stations direct, and then comes back to Scorpio as a Morning Star before leaving the sign on January 7, 2027. Along the way she repeatedly meets Pluto, Mars, and Jupiter, giving this cycle several chapters rather than one simple meaning.

The horoscopes below follow that entire arc through your Rising Sign. They are not meant to tell you what must happen. Astrology describes cycles and the kinds of experiences that may become meaningful within them. Sometimes an outer event activates an inner pattern. Sometimes a relationship becomes the mirror. Sometimes the change is quiet and happens in the way you understand yourself. The purpose of following the cycle is awareness, because awareness gives us more room to make conscious choices with whatever life places in front of us.

♈ARIES RISINGThe Merger, The Debt, and The Ex You Can't Forget+

September 10–October 2

Venus Direct in Scorpio, Evening Star

Venus enters Scorpio on September 10 and moves through your 8th House, and this is not casual territory. This is the House of vulnerability and intimacy, financial and emotional exchange, inheritance, debt, trust, sex, secrets, and the resources that become shared between people. Venus in Scorpio wants depth, loyalty, and emotional truth, and during this first part of the cycle you may feel a stronger desire to merge with someone, to know where you stand, or to find the place where you truly belong. The beautiful side of this is the possibility of forming deeper bonds and learning what real trust feels like. The harder side is that uncertainty can awaken anxiety, obsessive thinking, jealousy, or fears around rejection, dependency, loss, or emotional replacement. The 8th House often shows us that intimacy is not only about getting close to another person. It is also about what happens inside us when closeness makes us vulnerable enough that we can no longer control the outcome.

On September 15, Venus makes her first square to Pluto in Aquarius in your 11th House, bringing friendships, groups, alliances, audiences, and long-term goals into the story. Someone influential, wealthy, protective, intimidating, or emotionally powerful may enter through your social world, or a group dynamic may become much more important than expected. This can stir questions about loyalty, inclusion, usefulness, and what you believe you need to give in order to belong. You may want to feel necessary to the collective or important to the people who have access, status, or influence. Pluto can expose where belonging becomes entangled with fear of exclusion, and there is a spiritual lesson here around remembering that community is meant to support your path, not become the authority that decides your worth.

October 3–October 23

Venus Retrograde in Scorpio, Evening Star

On October 3, Venus stations retrograde at 8°29' Scorpio. Old entanglements can return with her. An ex may reappear and activate old wounds or old passions. Old debts can come due, and shared financial arrangements may require renegotiation. This is not my favorite period for casually lending large amounts of money, rushing into shared finances, signing a prenup without serious consideration, or making an irreversible decision because desire is speaking louder than judgment. Negotiate. Investigate. Read the fine print. What first appeared to be entirely about another person may begin turning inward as you recognize that the intensity of an attachment is not only about who they are, but what they represent to you: security, validation, belonging, protection, desire, or the hope that someone else can make uncertainty disappear.

On October 10, Venus retrograde squares Mars in Leo in your 5th House. Romance, pleasure, creativity, children, play, and personal desire collide with the 8th House need for intimacy and merging. Attraction can become exceptionally strong, but strong attraction is not the same as emotional safety. The 5th House wants joy, risk, romance, and the freedom to experience life through the heart, while the 8th wants to know how deep the bond goes and whether it can be trusted. What begins as pleasure can therefore expose much older questions around attachment and security. Then, on October 20, Venus makes her second square to Pluto. Friends, alliances, belonging, or future plans can reopen material around jealousy, emotional dependency, power, or fear of being left out. What feels intensely personal in the moment may also be touching an older place where acceptance became associated with emotional survival. Awareness does not make the feeling unreal; it simply gives you more choice about what you do with it.

October 23

Venus Kazimi

At the October 23 Kazimi, something about trust can become clearer. A secret may surface, a debt may be forgiven or called in, or you may acknowledge something about intimacy that you have been avoiding even with yourself. The Kazimi can bring an important clarification or an “aha” moment around what you actually want from another person and what deeper need has become attached to that desire. Not every realization solves the situation immediately, but seeing the pattern more clearly can be enough to begin responding to it differently.

October 25–November 13

Venus Retrograde in Libra, Morning Star

On October 25, Venus retrogrades into Libra and your 7th House, bringing marriage, committed relationships, business partnerships, best friends, open enemies, contracts, and one-to-one connections into focus. Venus is in domicile here and begins restoring perspective after Scorpio's emotional intensity. You may start asking not only how deeply you feel connected, but whether the relationship is mutual, fair, and capable of holding two separate people without one disappearing into the other. On November 4, Venus sextiles Jupiter in your 5th House and may bring romance, creativity, pleasure, affection, or renewed optimism. On November 10, Venus sextiles Mars there, and what was only attraction or inspiration can begin moving toward action. There is room here to enjoy another person without asking every pleasant moment to prove permanence, and that may be one of the healthiest adjustments of the entire cycle.

November 13–December 3

Venus Direct in Libra, Morning Star

Venus stations direct on November 13, and what has been reconsidered in relationship can begin moving forward. On November 28, Venus sextiles Jupiter again. A connection initiated or revisited during the retrograde may reveal that it does not fit the direction you are going, or it may finally have enough clarity to develop. You may understand more deeply that relationship does not need to rescue you from loneliness or prove your worth. Two people can remain whole and still choose one another, and sometimes that is a more profound form of intimacy than merging until neither person can tell where one ends and the other begins.

December 4–January 7

Venus Direct in Scorpio, Morning Star

On December 4, Venus returns to Scorpio and your 8th House. You are back in the same emotional territory you entered in September, but you are not standing in the same place internally. Now the question becomes whether what was recognized during the retrograde has actually been integrated. On December 9, Venus makes her third and final square to Pluto in your 11th House, and groups, friendships, alliances, or future goals may activate the material one more time. Aquarius asks for enough detachment to distinguish your personal emotional needs from the purpose of the collective. Detachment does not mean not caring. It means being able to participate without losing your center or making belonging responsible for your emotional survival.

On December 12, Venus sextiles Mars in Virgo in your 6th House, and ordinary things such as work, rest, health, routines, and care for the body can help ground what has been emotionally intense. Venus clears her retrograde shadow on December 15. Then, on January 3, 2027, Venus squares Jupiter in Leo in your 5th House, and you may again feel divided between the fear of vulnerability and the desire to enjoy romance, sex, creativity, children, play, and life through the heart. Venus in Scorpio wants loyalty and depth while Jupiter in Leo wants joy and expansion. Not every meaningful experience needs to promise permanence. Venus leaves Scorpio on January 7, and by then you may understand your emotional triggers around trust, loyalty, groups, and belonging more clearly. Trust does not mean knowing that nothing will ever be lost. It means developing enough inner security that connection can be experienced without having to control its outcome.

♉TAURUS RISINGThe Relationship, The Contract, and The Person Across from You+

September 10–October 2

Venus Direct in Scorpio, Evening Star

Venus enters Scorpio on September 10 and moves through your 7th House of marriage, committed partnership, business partnerships, contracts, close friendship, and the people who stand directly across from you. Venus is also your ruling planet, so this cycle can feel especially personal. Relationships may intensify quickly, and someone may want greater commitment, emotional access, honesty, exclusivity, or loyalty. Existing bonds can deepen through vulnerability, but jealousy, resentment, possessiveness, or control may also become more visible. The 7th House is where we meet the Other, yet it is also one of the places where we discover ourselves through projection. What you deeply admire, desire, resent, or fear in another person can reveal qualities and needs that are also alive within you. This does not make the other person imaginary; it simply means relationship can become one of the clearest mirrors through which the psyche learns about itself.

On September 15, Venus squares Pluto in Aquarius in your 10th House. Partnership and public life become entangled. Career, reputation, authority, professional ambition, or another person's goals may place pressure on the relationship, and a powerful professional connection may also become personally significant. You may find yourself trying to balance what a relationship requires privately with the person you are trying to become publicly. If a power struggle develops, it may be useful to notice not only who appears to hold the power, but what part of you is afraid of losing influence, approval, autonomy, or control. Sometimes the person across from us is carrying a conflict that already existed inside.

October 3–October 23

Venus Retrograde in Scorpio, Evening Star

On October 3, Venus stations retrograde in your 7th House, and relationships enter a period of revision. Former partners, unresolved agreements, old relationship patterns, or familiar emotional dynamics may return. You may begin asking whether what you expect another person to provide is something they can realistically give, or whether the relationship has become responsible for carrying needs that partly belong to you. This does not mean becoming emotionally self-sufficient to the point that you need nobody. It means recognizing the difference between healthy interdependence and asking another human being to repair every place where you feel uncertain, unseen, or unsafe.

On October 10, Venus squares Mars in Leo in your 4th House, pulling home, partnership, and career into a tense configuration. Family needs may conflict with a partner's needs or with your ambitions, and the interior and exterior parts of life can pull in different directions. You may be trying to preserve peace at home while another part of you wants movement, recognition, or greater independence. Then, on October 20, Venus makes her second square to Pluto in your 10th House. Questions involving career, reputation, influence, and power return. Another person's expectations may feel overwhelming, or your own ambitions may challenge the balance within a relationship. What looks like an external struggle can also expose an internal one between the need for closeness and the desire to remain in control of your own direction.

October 23

Venus Kazimi

At the October 23 Kazimi, something about partnership can become clearer. You may understand what another person wants, what you actually want from them, or why the relationship has developed the way it has. The clarification may also help you distinguish between the actual person in front of you and the role you have unconsciously asked them to play. Sometimes we relate not only to another human being, but also to the hopes, fears, and unfinished emotional material we have placed upon them. Seeing the difference can create much more room for an honest relationship.

October 25–November 13

Venus Retrograde in Libra, Morning Star

On October 25, Venus retrogrades into Libra and your 6th House, shifting attention toward work, health, responsibilities, self-care, and the balance between serving others and caring for yourself. Venus is strong in Libra and may help restore equilibrium after the intensity of the previous weeks. Sometimes relationship problems become much harder when everyone is exhausted, overworked, physically depleted, or carrying an unfair share of the ordinary labor of life. On November 4, Venus sextiles Jupiter in your 4th House and can bring more warmth into home and family life. On November 10, Venus sextiles Mars there, helping practical changes to routines or the home environment become easier to implement. There is a gentle spiritual truth here: love is not only what we feel in extraordinary moments. It is also how life is shared on an ordinary Tuesday.

November 13–December 3

Venus Direct in Libra, Morning Star

Venus stations direct on November 13, and what has been learned internally can begin to take form in everyday life. On November 28, another Venus-Jupiter sextile may bring cooperation around home, partnership, or family decisions. A healthier relationship is not simply one without conflict. It is one in which disagreement does not require either person to surrender individuality in order to restore peace, and where both people can contribute to a shared life without silently keeping score.

December 4–January 7

Venus Direct in Scorpio, Morning Star

On December 4, Venus returns to Scorpio and your 7th House. The relationship territory returns, but you now understand more about your expectations, projections, and emotional patterns. On December 9, Venus makes her final square to Pluto in your 10th House, and career, reputation, partnership, and questions of power meet again. You may be better able to recognize whether the tension belongs to the relationship itself or whether an older need for approval, respect, protection, or control is being activated through it. The person in front of you may still be difficult, but awareness gives you a better chance of responding to the actual person instead of only to what they represent.

On December 12, Venus sextiles Mars in Virgo in your 5th House, and romance, sex, pleasure, creativity, children, play, and joy can return more easily. The 5th House reminds you that relationship needs delight as well as seriousness, and that love is not only built through difficult conversations. Venus clears her shadow on December 15. On January 3, Venus squares Jupiter in your 4th House, and home and partnership needs may become exaggerated, particularly if neither person is willing to adjust. Because this is fixed energy, everyone may become convinced that their emotional position is the reasonable one. Venus leaves Scorpio on January 7. By then you may understand partnership less as something another person gives you and more as a living exchange between two people who continually reveal themselves to one another while still remaining themselves.

Venus enters Scorpio on September 10 and moves through your 6th House of work, health, coworkers, service, habits, responsibilities, and the systems that keep everyday life functioning. With Venus in Scorpio here, usefulness itself can become emotionally loaded, and it may be worth noticing whether being indispensable has quietly become one of the ways you try to feel valued.

On September 15, Venus squares Pluto in Aquarius in your 9th House. Your changing worldview may begin to conflict with everyday life. An “aha” moment around this can be enough to begin reorganizing life from a more conscious place.

October 25–November 13

Venus Retrograde in Libra, Morning Star

On October 25, Venus retrogrades into Libra and your 5th House. After the intensity of work and routine, the psyche may need romance, creativity, children, pleasure, play, and joy. December 4–January 7

On December 4, Venus returns to Scorpio and your 6th House. Venus clears her shadow on December 15. On January 3, Venus squares Jupiter in your 3rd House, and work, communication, relatives, learning, teaching, technology, or travel may demand more time. Be careful not to turn every available hour into productive time. Venus leaves Scorpio on January 7. A sustainable routine does not merely make you productive. It preserves enough of you that there is still someone left at the end of the day to enjoy the life all that work was supposed to support.

♌LEO RISINGThe House, The Family, and What Happens Behind Closed Doors+

Venus Direct in Scorpio, Evening Star

Venus enters Scorpio on September 10 and moves through your 4th House of home, family, parents, ancestry, property, emotional roots, and the private life that exists beneath the public personality. This can bring a stronger need to protect your inner world, beautify or reorganize your home, spend more time with family, or simply create a place where you can retreat and feel emotionally safe. But Scorpio rarely stays on the surface, and Venus here may also bring old family dynamics, resentment, loyalty, inheritance issues, jealousy, or emotional material that has been quietly living underneath the household atmosphere. The 4th House is not only the physical home. It is the emotional foundation we inherit before we are old enough to choose it, the family stories we absorb, and the places where belonging can become tangled with duty, memory, and expectation. Sometimes the deepest desire under this transit is very simple: to know that there is somewhere, and someone, where you can finally let your guard down.

On September 15, Venus squares Pluto in Aquarius in your 7th House, and partnership may begin activating that deeper family story. A partner may want changes to the home, or questions around living together, moving apart, marriage, property, family obligations, or emotional availability can become more important. Pluto in the 7th House can bring powerful people into your life, but it can also make relationships into mirrors for qualities you have not fully recognized in yourself. Another person's intensity, independence, control, distance, or emotional demands may stir something that feels bigger than the immediate relationship because it is touching an older pattern. This does not mean every relationship problem comes from childhood, but there are times when the present becomes the stage where unfinished emotional material from the past asks to be seen more consciously.

October 3–October 23

Venus Retrograde in Scorpio, Evening Star

On October 3, Venus stations retrograde in your 4th House, turning the attention inward toward home, family, emotional security, and your sense of belonging. You may reconsider where you live, how you live, who you share your space with, or what “home” actually means to you now. Family relationships may need revisiting, and old feelings can return even when you thought you had already moved beyond them. Sometimes what resurfaces is not a problem that needs to be fixed as much as an emotional pattern that needs to be understood. You may begin to recognize where loyalty has required too much silence, where peace has been maintained by avoiding difficult truths, or where you have confused familiarity with safety simply because something has always been part of your life.

On October 10, Venus retrograde squares Mars in Leo in your 1st House, and the need to protect your emotional roots collides with a much stronger desire to act for yourself. Mars in your own sign can make you more direct, more visible, more impatient with accommodation, and more willing to say what you need. With Pluto still in your 7th House, this creates a powerful tension between self, relationship, and home. You may feel caught between keeping the peace and asserting your individuality, or between what your family expects and what your relationship requires. Then, on October 20, Venus makes her second square to Pluto in your 7th House, bringing relationship material back into focus. A conflict with a partner may carry more emotional weight than the immediate situation seems to justify, or an old fear around abandonment, control, betrayal, or emotional power may become visible through the relationship. Sometimes another person presses on a wound they did not create. Awareness can help the relationship become the place where the old story is finally recognized instead of merely repeated.

October 23

Venus Kazimi

At the October 23 Kazimi, something about home, family, or your emotional needs may become clearer. You may understand whether you want to stay, move, reconcile, create distance, redefine a family relationship, or simply change the way you respond to what has been happening around you. The clarification may not solve every practical problem, but it can help you see which part of the situation belongs to the present and which part has been carried forward from an older emotional story. Sometimes an “aha” moment is simply the quiet realization that you no longer need to keep recreating the same version of home in order to feel that you belong.

October 25–November 13

Venus Retrograde in Libra, Morning Star

On October 25, Venus retrogrades into Libra and your 3rd House, and the emotional process begins looking for language. Conversations with siblings, relatives, neighbors, a partner, or people involved in property and household decisions may become important. Venus is strong in Libra here and can help bring more fairness and perspective into conversations that became emotionally charged while she was in Scorpio. On November 4, Venus sextiles Jupiter in Leo in your 1st House, restoring confidence, optimism, warmth, and the sense that you have choices. On November 10, Venus sextiles Mars there, and what has been discussed may finally become easier to act upon. Sometimes emotional healing is not one dramatic conversation. It is learning that your voice can exist in the room without either disappearing or having to overpower everyone else.

November 13–December 3

Venus Direct in Libra, Morning Star

Venus stations direct on November 13, and the conversations, decisions, or agreements that were being reconsidered may begin moving forward. By November 28, another sextile to Jupiter can bring greater confidence that you are capable of creating a private life that actually fits the person you are now. A helpful conversation with family or a partner may restore some emotional ease, or a practical decision involving home and relationships may finally make more sense. When you begin trusting your own voice, you may no longer need every family member or partner to completely understand your choices before you make them. Love does not always require agreement, and emotional maturity sometimes means accepting that people can care for one another while still needing very different things.

December 4–January 7

Venus Direct in Scorpio, Morning Star

On December 4, Venus returns to Scorpio and your 4th House. You are back in the territory of home, family, property, ancestry, and emotional roots, but you may now have a much clearer sense of what you actually need in order to feel safe. Some people may make changes to the home, move, reconcile with family, establish stronger boundaries, or simply recognize that an old emotional environment no longer feels like home in the same way.
