Horoscopes by Rising Sign

Virgo New Moon Cycle
Mercury → Libra · Venus → Scorpio

A precise reset. Where daily life meets devotion.

About this reading

Whole Sign Houses.
Read for your Rising.

These horoscopes are written for your Rising Sign using Whole Sign Houses. In this tradition, your Rising Sign becomes the 1st House, and each subsequent sign forms a complete House of life.

The Virgo New Moon initiates a practical cycle of refinement. It asks not for grand reinvention, but for precise, daily devotion to health, work, order, and the systems that hold your life together. With Mercury entering Libra and Venus entering Scorpio this week, conversations become diplomatic while desire and trust deepen. Find your Rising Sign below for where this reset lands.

♈6th House

Work, Health & Ritual

Aries Rising

The Virgo New Moon opens a new cycle in your 6th House of work, health, routines, service, and the systems that hold daily life together. This is a strong week to improve a schedule, develop a healthier practice, reorganize work, or address something that has become inefficient. Mercury enters your 7th House, placing more emphasis on conversations, agreements, and decisions involving partners or clients. Venus enters Scorpio in your 8th House, where intimacy, shared money, trust, and emotional vulnerability deepen. Relationship matters may require more honesty than politeness alone can provide. Practical improvements in your daily life can give you more capacity to handle the deeper conversations unfolding with others.

♉5th House

Creativity & Romance

Taurus Rising

The Virgo New Moon falls in your 5th House, opening a cycle around creativity, romance, pleasure, children, and the courage to express something that comes directly from you. A creative practice may need more discipline, or a romantic situation may become clearer when you pay attention to what is developing rather than what you hope will develop. Mercury enters your 6th House, supporting negotiations around work, schedules, health, and responsibilities. Venus enters Scorpio in your 7th House, making partnership one of the strongest areas of the week. Attraction and intimacy can deepen, but so can sensitivity around trust, power, and reciprocity. Relationships become more revealing when both people are willing to be emotionally honest.

♊4th House

Home & Foundations

Gemini Rising

The New Moon in Virgo begins a cycle in your 4th House of home, family, roots, and emotional foundations. You may feel ready to reorganize your living situation, address a family concern, or create more stability in your private life. Because Mercury rules this New Moon and enters Libra, attention also moves toward your 5th House of creativity, romance, pleasure, and self-expression. A conversation may help you understand what you want to create or experience next. Venus enters Scorpio in your 6th House, bringing greater intensity to work relationships, routines, health, or the balance between obligation and pleasure. The week favors practical changes at home that make more room for joy and creative life.

♋3rd House

Communication & Learning

Cancer Rising

The Virgo New Moon activates your 3rd House of communication, learning, writing, siblings, local life, and everyday decisions. A new conversation, study project, writing practice, or change in how you organize information can begin now. Mercury enters Libra in your 4th House, bringing attention to family conversations, home decisions, and the need for greater balance in your private environment. Venus enters Scorpio in your 5th House, deepening romance, creativity, pleasure, and emotional investment in what you love. Attraction can become more intense, and creative work may feel more personal. Your words carry weight this week, so use them to create clarity rather than simply discharge emotion.

♌2nd House

Money & Self-Worth

Leo Rising

The Virgo New Moon opens a new cycle in your 2nd House of money, resources, self-worth, and the way you support yourself materially. This is a useful time to examine spending, income, pricing, possessions, or the practical value of your skills. Mercury enters your 3rd House, increasing conversations, paperwork, learning, writing, and short-term planning. Venus enters Scorpio in your 4th House, drawing emotional attention toward home, family, privacy, and what gives you a sense of belonging. Financial decisions may connect with home or family needs. The deeper work is to build security in a way that reflects what you truly value rather than what simply looks impressive from the outside.

♍1st House

Self & Becoming

Virgo Rising

This New Moon occurs in your 1st House and places you personally at the center of the new cycle. Identity, body, presentation, priorities, and the direction you are choosing for yourself can all begin to shift. This is not a demand to reinvent your entire life. It is an opportunity to make a few precise changes that bring you into better alignment with who you are becoming. Mercury, your ruler, enters Libra and your 2nd House, directing attention toward income, values, self-worth, and practical decisions. Venus enters Scorpio in your 3rd House, making communication more emotionally charged and perceptive. Speak with honesty, but remember that intensity is not always the same as clarity.

♎12th House

Rest & Release

Libra Rising

The Virgo New Moon begins in your 12th House, making this a quieter and more internal new cycle. Rest, closure, spiritual practice, private reflection, and the release of habits that drain energy can become more important. Mercury enters Libra and your 1st House on the same day, so even while part of you is turning inward, your voice and mental presence become stronger. You may be ready to name something you have been processing privately. Venus, your ruler, enters Scorpio in your 2nd House, shifting attention toward money, values, self-worth, and what you are unwilling to compromise. The week can help you separate what truly nourishes you from what you have maintained simply to preserve harmony.

♏11th House

Community & Future

Scorpio Rising

The Virgo New Moon activates your 11th House of friendships, networks, community, audiences, and future plans. A goal may become more concrete, or you may recognize which people and groups genuinely support the future you are trying to build. Mercury enters Libra in your 12th House, suggesting that some thinking or planning is still happening privately. Not every idea needs immediate explanation. Venus enters Scorpio and your 1st House, increasing magnetism, desire, self-awareness, and attention to relationship. Because Venus also moves into a more intense sequence with Chiron and Pluto, what you want may feel especially important. Let desire reveal information about you without allowing it to make every decision for you.

♐10th House

Career & Reputation

Sagittarius Rising

The Virgo New Moon begins a new cycle in your 10th House of career, reputation, achievement, and public direction. This is one of the strongest Rising signs for a professional reset this week. A new responsibility, system, goal, role, or strategy may begin to take shape. Mercury enters Libra in your 11th House, emphasizing networking, collaboration, audiences, friendships, and the people who can help move an idea forward. Venus enters Scorpio in your 12th House, making part of your emotional or relationship life more private. Feelings may be deeper than you are ready to show publicly. The week favors practical career movement while allowing private emotional material the time it needs to become clear.

Mercury enters Libra in your 9th House, supporting learning, teaching, writing, publishing, travel, and a broader perspective. Venus enters Scorpio in your 10th House, bringing relationships, values, aesthetics, money, or diplomacy into career and public life. ♓7th House

Partnership & Contracts

Pisces Rising

The Virgo New Moon occurs in your 7th House of marriage, partnership, clients, contracts, and significant one-to-one relationships. A relationship may enter a new phase, or you may become clearer about what a workable partnership actually requires. Mercury enters Libra in your 8th House, encouraging conversations about intimacy, trust, shared money, obligations, or subjects that are not always easy to discuss openly. Venus enters Scorpio in your 9th House, deepening your attraction to travel, learning, spirituality, teaching, or experiences that expand your emotional world. Partnership is central this week, but the most useful beginning is not simply getting closer. It is becoming more precise about what mutuality, trust, and shared responsibility mean to you.
