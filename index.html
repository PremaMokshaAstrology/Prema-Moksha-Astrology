About Me

Astrologer, therapist, and lifelong student of the cosmos

Welcome

I found astrology around the age of twelve, during a time when I was trying to understand the roots of karma and suffering and the occurrences that appeared so unfair. I was looking for meaning and for a deeper understanding of life. What began as a search for answers became a lifelong journey into astrology and the mysteries of human experience.

My formal studies began in 1998 at the Institute Ganesha of Healing Practices in Rio de Janeiro, where I studied Astro-Psychology based on the work of Liz Greene and the psychological astrology tradition known as Modern Astrology. This was my first formal introduction to astrology as a way of understanding the inner world, our emotional patterns, our struggles, and the deeper layers of who we are.

For many years, astrology was something I practiced mostly with friends and family. I worked with natal charts, transits, and progressions, while continuing to study and deepen my understanding of the subject. Over time, astrology became much more than something I was interested in. It became a way of looking at life, understanding our experiences, and recognizing the connection between our inner world and the larger cycles of existence. In 2023, I decided to bring this lifelong path into my professional work and expand my knowledge to people outside my community.

My approach has also been shaped by more than twenty years of study in therapeutic disciplines, including Traditional Chinese Medicine, yoga, and Ayurveda. These traditions taught me to look at the whole person and to recognize the subtle connections between body, mind, emotions, and spirit. They have deeply influenced the way I work with astrology and the way I approach each chart.

Today, my work brings together psychological astrology, traditional techniques, predictive astrology, and the knowledge I have gathered through many years of exploring healing, consciousness, and the human experience. At the heart of my work is a simple idea: that our lives are part of something much larger than ourselves, and that the sky above us has a language of its own. My role is to help interpret that language.

Thank you for being here. I invite you to explore astrology with me as a way of understanding yourself, your life, and your place within the greater mystery of the Cosmos.
