---
title: "Finding the Ground Where We Can Grow"
date: "2026-10-09"
category: "Astrology & Self-Awareness"
---

Today, I am thankful. There were moments when the actions of others left me feeling as though the ground had been pulled from beneath my feet. I lost connections, opportunities, and a sense of belonging. Yet somewhere in that fall, I felt held by the universe. Slowly, I found places where what I have to offer is welcomed and can grow.

The two businesses I am part of are succeeding and expanding. The people working alongside me have shown kindness, openness, and a willingness to support one another. I am remembering how good it feels to contribute without having to brace myself for competition or protect my place in the room.

The longing to be loved and accepted is deeply human. When that longing becomes tangled with fear, another person’s gifts can feel like a threat. We may hide parts of ourselves to remain agreeable, or undermine someone else to feel more secure. These patterns deserve honest reflection, including when we recognize them in ourselves. Understanding the hurt beneath a behavior can invite compassion while still leaving room for boundaries.

As a trauma survivor, I know how deeply personal attacks can affect me. Sometimes my body responds before I have had time to understand what I am feeling. I become defensive, overwhelmed, or pulled toward reactions that do not reflect how I want to relate. I am learning to meet those responses with compassion and to take responsibility for what I do with them. Part of that responsibility is choosing environments where I do not have to remain constantly on guard.

I need relationships where concerns can be spoken about directly, differences can exist without punishment, and misunderstandings can be met with a willingness to repair. I need to feel that being myself will not cost me my belonging.

This awareness also matters in the ways we care for children. What remains unexamined in us can influence how we respond to their feelings, their needs, and their growing independence. Inner work gives us an opportunity to notice what we learned to survive and consider what we want to pass on. We do not have to become perfect parents. There is meaning in learning to pause, apologize, listen, and respond differently.

This is one reason I value astrology as a language for self-awareness. Reflecting on a chart can offer questions about our emotional needs, our defenses, and the patterns we bring into relationships. For me, that reflection belongs alongside the work of listening to the body, noticing tension, and giving feelings time and space to be understood. Insight becomes more meaningful when we can gradually bring it into the way we live.

I have lost a great deal, but I have also gained something precious by moving toward places where my gifts are welcomed. There is healing in being appreciated for what comes naturally to us. There is healing in discovering that connection can feel supportive, even while everyone involved is still learning and carrying something difficult.

For me, safety has come to mean consistency I can trust. Words that match actions. Kindness that remains when there is a disagreement. The possibility of making a mistake and still being treated with care. Even when my inner world feels unsettled, these experiences help me find my footing.
