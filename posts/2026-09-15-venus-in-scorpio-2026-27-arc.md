## Phase 1: Evening Star Direct

**Sept 10, 2026 @ 8:10pm ET — Venus enters Scorpio at 0°**  
She is Evening Star, visible after sunset.

In this phase, Venus meets Pluto in a square and intensifies interpersonal relations. Truth-telling comes out. But truth is personal, and there may be intense disagreements over what is true. As the Moon joins the square, it makes everything personal and emotional.

## Phase 2: Shadow & Station Retrograde

**Oct 3, 2026 @ 8:13pm ET — Station Retrograde at 8°29' Scorpio**

Revisions of the results of any transformation by Venus aspects to Moon and Pluto will occur during this period. Here we go within and integrate all emotional baggage and deep transformations from the previous periods.

## Phase 3: Kazimi / Inferior Conjunction

**Oct 24, 2026 @ 4:00am UTC / Oct 23 11pm ET — Venus conjunct Sun at ∼1° Scorpio**  
Heart of the Sun.

This is the official death and rebirth. Old Venus cycle ends, new one begins. Some clarity sinks in, some realizations. The truth within the matter is found.

## Phase 4: Morning Star Retrograde

**Oct 24–25, 2026 — Venus now Morning Star retrograde, but still in Scorpio. She goes back into Libra on Oct 25**

This is the integration period. At this point, we have removed what no longer belongs, transformed the pain, integrated the wisdom, and are shining in the morning. When Venus goes into her own sign, Libra, she is empowered and ready for the liberation truth brought. But this slow build-up of empowerment is not yet moving towards the outer world but is a silent renewal period.

## Phase 5: Morning Star Direct

**Nov 13–14, 2026 — Stations direct at 22°51' Libra**

**Dec 4, 2026 – Jan 7, 2027 — Re-enters Scorpio as Morning Star Direct**

Here, Venus is empowered, direct, and strong. Finances, harmony, and love get a boost. When it re-enters Scorpio, the journey can now allow for health, intimacy, belonging, and inner power.

Scorpio is the alchemist, and during this period, lead is transformed into gold. The friction with overpowering exterior situations makes us question how much we want what we want and how much we value what we value. The transformation may have hurt by creating the sensation of losing something important. But loss is not the right word. We can't lose what is ours. The transformations are always for our highest benefit.

This is a tough period for our emotions. But, a diamond would not be a diamond without pressure.
