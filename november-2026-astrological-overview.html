A Closing Reflection

November begins with Mercury and Venus retrograde, bringing old conversations, memories and feelings back into our awareness. There is something about these cycles that brings our shadows out to play, revealing our fears, insecurities and the ways we protect ourselves from being vulnerable. As both planets turn direct, we may begin to understand some of these experiences differently. Meanwhile, Mars and Jupiter together in Leo bring confidence, enthusiasm and the desire to act, but also the possibility of exaggeration, pride and conflict. Saturn in Aries is asking us to examine our self-protection mechanisms and rebuild boundaries after its long passage through Pisces, when so many of those boundaries became difficult to recognize. The growing harmony between Jupiter and Saturn reminds us that expansion also requires responsibility.

In the larger world, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto continue their collaboration. Pluto in Aquarius speaks of the power of people organizing around shared principles and a vision of the future. Uranus in Gemini adds speed, technology and intelligence, while Neptune in Aries brings faith and conviction, but also the possibility of deep disagreements when beliefs become more important than our ability to understand one another. I see in these planetary relationships something of the younger generations rising up, questioning established structures and looking for a different future. There is great potential for progress, but also a struggle over what that future should look like.

Toward the end of November, the Full Moon in Gemini, close to Uranus, may bring information to light, unexpected conversations or a different understanding of situations that have been developing for some time. As Mars moves into Virgo, there is a growing need for discernment, responsibility and attention to the details we might otherwise overlook. Our vulnerability may be challenged, our beliefs questioned and some relationships may require a deeper kind of honesty. Perhaps the most valuable thing we can do is pay attention to our reactions, recognize the patterns that repeat themselves and become a little more conscious of how we participate in the world around us.
