An Astrological Session with Me

What to expect, how it works, and how to prepare

An astrological consultation begins with you. Before interpreting your chart, I want to understand what led you to seek astrological support, what you are experiencing, and what you hope to understand more deeply. You are welcome to share only what feels comfortable. Even a little knowledge of your story, relationships, career, questions, and present circumstances helps me connect the symbolism of your chart with your reality. Please see the guidelines for sharing below.

Natal Chart Analysis

In a Natal Chart Analysis, this information allows me to guide the report toward a deeper understanding of who you are and understand the alignment with your astrological promise. The chart describes your natural dispositions, inner tensions, strengths, developmental possibilities, and the patterns that may accompany you throughout life. When its symbolism is connected with your lived experience, the interpretation becomes more personal, practical, and supportive.

Transit Consultation

During a Transit Consultation, we can concentrate on the areas of life you most want to understand. Your questions help me identify which natal placements, planetary alignments, and patterns of sacred geometry are most active during that period. Transits can reveal the hand of fate moving through a particular time, while awareness allows you to meet that time more consciously, make meaningful choices, and use your will wisely.

Progression Consultation

A Progression Consultation follows a similar approach. By learning about you and beginning with the foundation of your natal chart, we can delineate a timeline of your inner development and recognize the cycles through which your life has unfolded. This reveals how you have developed from the Natal Promise into the person you are today and how your current cycles may continue to unfold in the near future.

For your first Progression Consultation, the written report will cover your cycles from birth to the present, as well as the coming year. Yearly follow-up Progression Consultations are shorter and are therefore offered at the same price as a Zoom consultation.

Follow-Up Consultations

Follow-Up Consultations are shorter, more focused sessions that can be scheduled whenever a new question arises, when you would like to explore a particular astrological influence of the moment, or when you wish to continue your astrological self-inquiry.

Astrology is the study of celestial cycles and their synchronicity with human cycles. By understanding how these cycles move together, you gain a wider perspective and the wisdom to recognize the nature of a particular period, respond more consciously, and make choices that support your development.

Before We Begin

When hiring my services, please send me a message introducing yourself and sharing a little about the four central pillars of your life. You may write as much or as little as feels comfortable.

You and Your Personal Development

Tell me something about who you are, how you see yourself, how you feel others perceive you, your relationship with your body, and your instinctive way of responding to life.

Home, Family, and Roots

You may share something about your family, upbringing, roots, ancestry, early emotional experiences, or childhood wounds. This helps me connect the symbolism of your chart with the experiences that have shaped your inner foundation.

Relationships and Partnerships

Tell me about the important one-to-one relationships in your life. These may include marriage, romantic relationships, business partnerships, closest friendships, or other meaningful and lasting connections.

Career and Reputation

Share something about your work, vocation, ambitions, public life, and the reputation you are building. You may also tell me about your present professional questions, changes, or aspirations.

You are always in control of what you choose to share. These details simply allow me to move beyond a general interpretation and create astrological guidance that speaks more directly to your life, your questions, and the choices before you.
